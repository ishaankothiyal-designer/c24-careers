# Role sticker redesign: concept before artwork · 15 November 2026

The user rejected the original56px treatment as too small and too heavily outlined, and asked for better Flatland concepts plus a compact-screen overlap assessment. This supersedes the earlier dark-outline miniature icon direction.

## Shared creative rules

Use one coherent illustration family with generous colour shapes, restrained1.5–2-unit muted-purple contours, a very light lilac paper offset and a quiet cream edge. Retain the Flatland violet/lilac/green/cream palette. Avoid heavy black rims, dense double outlines, large certification ticks, excessive UI lines, lettering,3D effects and unrelated embellishment. Give each subject equal optical prominence at a larger84px desktop/tablet canvas. Native SVG generation is through Higgsfield GPT Image2.6; the generated sheet is reviewed before adoption.

## Layout decision

Remove the old negative-margin sticker placement. Larger art must occupy an actual footer row, clear of the summary and CTA. Hide it on phone viewports through767px and inside any card whose content area is260px or narrower; the existing action row then returns to its text-only footprint. Preserve card insets and body type. Do not compress text, shrink gutters or overlap the divider to force artwork onto compact devices.
