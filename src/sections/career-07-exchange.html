An honest exchange

Know what the work asks of you.

Some people want a clear ladder and a conventional title. Others want harder problems and more ambiguity. Both are fair choices.

  • Agency comes with consequence. Own the decision, explain your reasoning, and learn openly when a sincere call fails.
  • Flat can fail. Call it out. When informal hierarchy gets in the way, bring the gap into the conversation and help change it.
  • Some work keeps its hierarchy. Some operations need clear decision rights and accountability. The way the work runs will vary by role.
