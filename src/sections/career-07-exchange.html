Know what the work asks of you

You’ll have room to make decisions. We’ll ask you to explain your reasoning, speak up when something gets in the way, and understand how different teams work.

  1. 01

    Agency comes with consequence

    Own the decision, explain your reasoning, and learn openly when a sincere call fails.

  2. 02

    Flat can fail; call it out

    When informal hierarchy gets in the way, bring the gap into the conversation and help change it.

  3. 03

    Some work keeps its hierarchy

    Some operations need clear decision rights and accountability. The way the work runs will vary by role.
