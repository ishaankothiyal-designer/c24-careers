Learning, building, and sharing with AI.

Three snapshots of how teams are putting AI to work, from the ChatGPT pilot to a two-day build session and HTMLHub. Each figure describes its own initiative and reporting period.

From around 20 to over 200 people.

In June 2025, around 20 people were using agentic workflows. By October 2026, more than 200 people had started using them.

Across teams, people are bringing AI into everyday work—from finance and IT support to employee operations and page authoring.
