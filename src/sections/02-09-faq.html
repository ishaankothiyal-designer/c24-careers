No. Apply for the role that fits your skills. “Create 100 founders” is an ambition for the opportunities we create inside Cars24, not a requirement for candidates.

Start with the problem on the card, then read the full role description and requirements. Use the hiring conversations to agree on the scope, the decisions you can make and the outcomes you’ll own.

Growth can mean taking a problem from an idea to a working tool. Nishant built a financial consolidation tool and deployed it on Launchpad during the Finance AI war room. Open the builder stories to see more examples.

Each opening lists its locations and any published work arrangement. Check the role details and confirm the office, hub, field or remote expectations with your hiring contact.

The process varies by role. Ask your hiring contact about the stages, who you’ll meet and the expected timeline. Be ready to discuss something you built: the problem, your decisions and what changed.

People organise around problems to solve. The aim is to keep context close to the work, make decisions without unnecessary layers and take responsibility for outcomes. You don’t need a particular title to contribute.
